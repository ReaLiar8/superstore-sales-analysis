# Superstore Sales Analysis
## About:
SQL analysis of 9,800 retail sales orders from the Superstore dataset.

## Dataset:
Source: [yashadafunde-04/superstore-sales-analysis](https://github.com/yashadafunde-04/superstore-sales-analysis)

## Analyses:
- Top 10 products by sales
- Demand on products categories
- Amount of sold products and average sales from each city in California
- States with the most consumer customers
- Customers from Kentucky with sales over 500

## Tools:
- SQL (SQLite)
- DB Browser for SQLite
